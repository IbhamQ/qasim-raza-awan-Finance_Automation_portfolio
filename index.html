My process

How I approach a project.

Understand the process, then build the controls.

Automation is only useful when the underlying logic can be explained and trusted. I start by understanding the accounting flow, map discrepancies to root causes, build reproducible checks, and make exceptions visible to the people who can resolve them.

That approach works across supplier reconciliation, receivables, fee compliance, reporting pipelines and management dashboards.
