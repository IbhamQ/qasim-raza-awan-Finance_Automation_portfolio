Behind the work

Finance professional. Systems thinker.

I work at the intersection of accounting, operational data and automation. My background spans accounts receivable, credit control, banking operations, financial reporting and revenue/cost reconciliation. Across those roles, I kept finding the same problem: talented people spending hours on tasks that well-designed systems could complete in minutes.

I combine accounting knowledge with SQL, Python, Power BI, APIs, webhooks, Excel and Apps Script to build connected workflows: reconciliations, exception alerts, scheduled reporting, dashboards and financial models. I also work with automation patterns that can be implemented using tools such as Zapier, Make and n8n.
